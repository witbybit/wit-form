## What this shows

Fields listed in [`depFields`](/docs/api/use-field#depfields) arrive in the validator's second argument as `other.values`, and the field re-validates whenever they change.

- The confirmation re-validates when the new password changes.
- `PasswordChecklist` uses [`useFieldWatch`](/docs/api/watch-hooks#usefieldwatch) to follow one field without re-rendering the form.
- [`reinitializeOnSubmit`](/docs/api/use-form#reinitializeonsubmit) clears the form after a successful submit.
