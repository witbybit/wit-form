## What this shows

- `useForm({ schema })` takes any [Standard Schema](https://standardschema.dev): Zod, Valibot, ArkType, Effect Schema, or Yup 1.7 and newer. There's no adapter to install. See [Schema validation](/docs/guides/validation#schema-validation).
- Each issue goes to the field at its path. `travelers[1].age` lands on the age field of the second traveler row, and `contact.email` on the email field.
- An issue for the list itself ("Add at least one traveler") becomes the field array's `error`. Remove every traveler to see it.
- The `.refine` rule has no path, so it becomes a form error. The form shows it from `formState.formErrors`.
- The schema runs again as values change, so errors clear as soon as you fix them. As with any error, a field shows its error once it's touched.

The `TextField` and `NumberField` components come from the [shared example components](/docs/examples#shared-components).
