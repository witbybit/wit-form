## What this shows

The order summary isn't inside the form's `FormProvider`. The form has a `formId` and `skipJotaiProvider: true`, so its state lives in a shared Jotai store, and the summary passes the same `formId` to `useFormValues` and `useFormContext`. See [Using a form outside its provider](/docs/api/form-provider#using-a-form-outside-its-provider).
